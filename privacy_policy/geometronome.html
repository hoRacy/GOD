Music features work offline without an in-app account. The phone app displays Google AdMob ads unless PREMIUM is active. Google may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data according to privacy choices and its policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Manage ad and analytics choices in Settings → Privacy settings when the Google UMP form is available. Google Play handles purchases and payments; the app receives purchase status and identifiers. Export creates a video with synthetic audio in the cache. Sharing sends it only to an app you choose at your request; that app handles it under its own policies. External links open sites with their own privacy policies.

Privacy questions: horacy007@gmail.com.

The phone and watch automatically send crash, unresponsive app and selected error reports to Sentry, including the app and OS versions, device model and a short history of actions. We do not attach microphone audio, click track names or contents, screenshots or session recordings. Performance monitoring and profiling are disabled. Manual feedback includes your description and optional contact email. Sending requires internet; reports may temporarily wait on the device. Service provider: Sentry (sentry.io/privacy).
